Reconciled results.
Every account, every cycle.

Bank lines are matched to ledger entries by amount, date, and counterparty, with fuzzy tolerances tuned per property.

Anything that does not tie out is surfaced with a confidence score and a one line reason, ranked by how much it moves the balance.

Each match cites the statement line and the ledger row behind it, so an override takes a glance rather than an investigation.

When two ledger entries could both explain a line, Tieout shows the candidates side by side instead of guessing.

Unmapped bank fees are grouped so you can route them to a GL account once, and future cycles apply it automatically.

Once you sign off, approved entries post and exceptions are flagged back in Yardi, with an audit trail on every change.

The close package writes itself: totals, match rate, resolved exceptions, and the reasoning behind each posting.
